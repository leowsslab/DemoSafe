# AI collaboration and media disclosure

DemoSafe is a one-person entry developed with an AI assistant. The assistant
supported implementation, tests, fictional fixture generation, actual browser
operation, video editing, slides, captions and documentation. The human entrant
makes project decisions and handles their own accounts, eligibility, review and
submission information. AI is a tool, not an additional team member.

The prototype uses existing RapidOCR / PP-OCRv4 models. We do not claim to have
trained or invented them. Our contribution is the local review workflow,
temporal evidence, explicit masking decisions, access controls and reproducible
evaluation. All supplied test interfaces and values are fictional. They do not
represent customer adoption or measured commercial impact.

The participant supplied the English ElevenLabs narration. Its 351-word script
was checked by local transcription. The v4 film places the approximately
152.84-second source once in a 156-second timeline, without internal cuts,
added internal silence or tempo changes. No replacement voice was generated.
The filename identifies Chris; the exact generation model and applicable
account entitlement are not independently established by the file itself.
Publication authorization does not constitute verification of those facts.
The source voice is kept outside this repository.

Real browser operation was captured at approximately 1-4 frames per second.
The 30 fps delivery repeats those captures; it is not native 30 fps recording.
Output rescanning uses the same OCR and is not an independent privacy guarantee.
UGen300 inference, customer adoption, revenue and guaranteed redaction are not
claimed. Third-party code, models and fonts retain their individual licenses.
